The briefwhat was asked

I was challenged to produce an inventive, high-concept short film entitled “The Meeting.” I could choose any genre, and the plot was entirely open-ended. However, I had to actively steer clear of tired student film clichés, specifically low-budget gangster/crime dramas and generic horror/jumpscare setups. Instead, I had to focus on subtle, original human dynamics, unexpected premise twists, surreal encounters, tense negotiations or clever comedy.
