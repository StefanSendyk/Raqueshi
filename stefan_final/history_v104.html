• {img:v104/hist_robinhood.jpg|Still from The Adventures of Robin Hood (1938)}1900s–40s · First thrillsThe Great Train Robbery (1903) is one of the first films with a robbery, a chase and a shoot-out. Swashbucklers like The Adventures of Robin Hood (1938) turn sword fights and stunts into big-screen spectacle.
  • {img:v104/hist_sevensamurai.jpg|Still from Seven Samurai (1954)}1950s · Westerns & samuraiWesterns rule: High Noon (1952) plays out almost in real time against a ticking clock. In Japan, Kurosawa's Seven Samurai (1954) creates the “assemble the team” story and the slow-motion death.
  • {img:v103/hist_drno.jpg|Still from Dr. No (1962)}1960s · The spy arrivesDr. No (1962) sets the template for the modern spy film: suits, gadgets, a villain with a plan. Le Samouraï (1967) brings the silent, stylish hitman and Bullitt (1968) the modern car chase.
  • {img:v104/hist_enterdragon.jpg|Still from Enter the Dragon (1973)}1970s · Grit & martial artsTough city cops in The French Connection and Dirty Harry (both 1971). Bruce Lee's Enter the Dragon (1973) makes martial arts popular all over the world.
  • {img:v103/hist_diehard.jpg|Still from Die Hard (1988)}1980s · The blockbuster heroFamous heroes, bigger stunts and fights: Rocky, Raiders of the Lost Ark (1981), The Terminator (1984). Die Hard (1988) traps an ordinary man in one building.
  • {img:v103/hist_matrix.jpg|Still from The Matrix (1999)}1990s · Style & CGIHong Kong gunfights (Hard Boiled, 1992) and Tarantino's talkative hitmen (Pulp Fiction, 1994) change the tone. CGI and complex editing bring new visual styles, led by The Matrix (1999).
  • {img:v104/hist_darkknight.jpg|Still from The Dark Knight (2008)}2000s · Real & rawHandheld camera and fast cuts make fights feel real in The Bourne Identity (2002). Kill Bill (2003) goes stylised, and The Dark Knight (2008) adds a moral dilemma.
  • {img:v104/hist_madmax.jpg|Still from Mad Max: Fury Road (2015)}2010s · Practical againLong, clear fight choreography and real stunts: John Wick (2014), Mad Max: Fury Road (2015), and Baby Driver (2017), cut to its soundtrack.
  • {img:v104/hist_topgun.jpg|Still from Top Gun: Maverick (2022)}2020s · TodayReal stunts mixed with CGI, ambitious camera work and more complex heroes: Extraction (2020), Top Gun: Maverick (2022), Everything Everywhere All at Once (2022).
