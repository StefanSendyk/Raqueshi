01 — About me
YOUR PHOTO HERE

We can never truly be free. We are constantly bound by the law, society, and ultimately by ourselves. For if there are no chains attached to a man, there is no man.

The media is one of the primary architects of these social and psychological chains. It is the defining force of every living generation, shaping our modern understanding of good and bad, right and wrong. Because the media is such an efficient vehicle for relaying ideas, its influence is not always positive. This power is exactly where my passion for media studies begins. I am deeply fascinated by the psychological effects used to make narratives look and sound convincing, as well as the camera and audio techniques deployed to induce raw emotions like sadness or fear.

My goalMy goal is to master these tools to truly understand how media shapes the human experience.
